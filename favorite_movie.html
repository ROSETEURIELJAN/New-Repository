RANGO MOVIE

RANGO

INTRODUCTION

A western, animated talking animal movie that has a gritty aesthetic, with a 7.3 ★ rating on IMDb

PLOT

After getting tossed into the harsh desert, a pet chameleon survives through sheer luck. Meeting with an iguana named Beans and traveling to the town 'Dirt', he is appointed sheriff by the Mayor and is tasked with solving the missing water.

CAST

Johnny Depp / Rango

A pet chameleon thrust into the wilderness, only to become something in the town of 'Dirt'

Isla Fisher / Beans

A desert iguana living in "Dirt', which is not a great place for intellect and beauty

Ned Beatty / Mayor

A wheelchair bound tortoise, overseeing 'Dirt' and its water crisis

Gil Birmingham / Wounded Bird

A crow from a native tribe, the voice of reason and bringer of bad news

REVIEW

Rango is a return to form, a love letter to the western genre as a whole. This movie is one you must see for yourself, as the story, the unconventional artstyle and inventive designs of the models, and it's tones of finding one self through life are superb.
